# Complete-history Consent adapters

The developing CompleteHistory profile has separate source, validation and grant-use
adapters for collections whose original binding history names different Artists.
These adapters do not activate a hydration route or advertise a new owner capability.
Their integration, native execution, size and seven-owner acceptance remain pending.

The existing generation, dispute and PRIMARY_ONLY collaborator profiles keep their
current selectors, admission rules and byte encodings. The complete-history caller
must first authenticate the common Binding, Archive, Identity, nonce and scope
inventory. Its collection selectors describe current heads, including pending or
terminal heads; each historical record retains its actual original principal.
A genuinely unbound collection has no binding or Consent records.

## Source and transport

`StreamArtistCompleteHistoryConsentSource.collect(Context)` takes the fixed Consent
source, full scope, common `StreamArtistCompleteHistoryTypes.Inventory`, and the
ordered economics and royalty-freeze terms for each collection. It returns one
shared Consent supplement row per collection. The terms follow that collection's
original operation-15 and operation-20 journal order across all historical Artists.

The collector authenticates the complete source owner-6 certificate. It reads each
original record and its delegation association, uses the original receipt's Artist
for historical economics and royalty lookups, and checks current sales, content,
freeze and ratification heads. It never reconstructs a filtered owner certificate.
`requireCurrent` repeats collection and compares the complete encoded result.

Original operation-52 records retain only their three stored fields: record hash,
content-state hash and metadata contract. No generation, signer, nonce or timestamp
is added. Their original Identity evidence is joined separately. An empty original
ratification head must remain empty, including for an unbound collection.

When native operation 12 exists, the fixed aggregate-sanction collector reads the
actual global Archive catalogue once, including original zero-native operation 13.
The resulting canonical `H.Inventory` occupies only the first shared supplement
row. That row is a transport location, not the owner of the global evidence.
The caller must preserve the fixed sanction source/currentness and original Identity
checks. A different catalogue hash domain cannot substitute for this inventory.

## Validation and conservation

`StreamArtistCompleteHistoryConsentValidation.validate` receives decoded Consent
rows, ratifications, full scope/common inventory and canonical sanction bytes.
It joins every supplied binding to the authenticated historical binding inventory,
matches each record to the complete original journal, and proves one global
revision/replay/alias census, including operation-13 mutations without native
receipts. The current Artist is never substituted for a historical record's Artist.

Original operations 15, 16, 17, 20 and 21 carry their actual saved generation and
must match that generation's accepted binding and principal. Original 14 has no
stored generation: a delegated policy record requires an accepted historical
mode-2 binding for its original Artist, without assigning the record a generation.
Original 52 likewise does not acquire a generation during transport.

`StreamArtistCompleteHistoryConsentUses.validate(Context)` consumes canonical
Identity rows aligned with the complete principal union, the original scope,
Consent rows and full provenance. It returns increments by original Artist and
retained grant. Every original Consent receipt must find its exact retained
Identity signature row; direct/Safe empty signature bytes remain valid evidence.
Operations 12, 13 and 52 add no delegation uses. The enclosing profile combines
these increments with other families and performs the final grant-use equality
once. Identity authentication, nonce completeness and other families remain
required prerequisites.

## Evidence boundaries

Focused tests use complete synthetic owner-6 certificates and canonical Identity
frames. Source tests bind exact typed getter responses and explicitly mock the
admitted-owner boundary; unmatched getter arguments fail. They exercise historical
Artist selection, original rows, pending/unbound heads, replay and grant accounting,
but do not establish real Core, Safe, source admission or seven-owner import.
Source and ABI evidence must not be represented as native execution or release
acceptance. Combined current-stack, constructor/runtime capacity and rollback
qualification belong to the integrated profile.
